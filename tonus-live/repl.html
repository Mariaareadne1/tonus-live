welcome

You've found étude, a live-coding language that reads like the part on your music stand: notes, bar lines, dynamics, bowings and hairpins.

1. hit play – 2. change something – 3. hit update

The code on the left is a beat written in étude. Try changing bowing pizz to bowing legato, or dynamics pp mf p mf to dynamics pp < ff, then press ctrl+enter.

New here? The workshop walks through the language one effect at a time.

étude is also a little programming language: name a theme with let, loop it with repeat 4 as n:, transpose it with up n, and change the ending with if n is last then …. Load to hear all of it.

keys

  • ctrl+enter play / update
  • ctrl+. stop
  • ctrl+z undo
